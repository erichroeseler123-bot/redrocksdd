HOW TO GET HOME FROM RED ROCKS AFTER A CONCERT

Driving, rideshare, taxis, shuttles, friend pickup and a prearranged driver all work differently at the end of a Red Rocks show. Here is the short answer first, then the right deep-dive when you need it.

The 20-second answer

At the end of an event, Red Rocks directs passenger pickup by private vehicles, taxis and Uber/Lyft to the Jurassic Lot near Entrance 2. The venue recommends the Will Call Trail across from the Trading Post and then Red Rocks Park Road to reach the pickup area. If you do not want to search for a ride after the show, a prearranged driver is the option where the driver is already onsite — although every vehicle can still encounter Red Rocks exit traffic.

Your main ways home

There is no single best answer for every group. The useful question is what you want solved before the music starts.

Drive yourself

Simple and usually the lowest direct cost. The tradeoff is that someone in your group has to stay able to drive and you still leave with venue traffic.

Uber or Lyft

Available, but end-of-show pickup is centralized at the Jurassic Lot and prices are dynamic. Read the full Uber/Lyft guide →

Taxi

Taxis use the same official end-of-event passenger pickup area as rideshare. Availability and fare depend on the operator and the night.

Commercial shuttle

Useful if you are comfortable with a fixed departure point and schedule. Do not confuse commercial transportation with the venue's complimentary internal park shuttles.

Friend or family pickup

Your driver should follow the same end-of-event pickup instructions: passenger pickup is directed to the Jurassic Lot near Entrance 2.

Prearranged driver

The benefit is not magic traffic avoidance. It is that you are not opening an app after the show hoping a new driver appears. Your transportation plan already exists.

Is Uber available after Red Rocks?

Yes. Red Rocks specifically identifies Uber/Lyft as ride services using the Jurassic Lot for end-of-event pickup. The harder questions are what the fare will be, how long matching will take and how busy the pickup area will be on your particular night.

Where do taxis, Uber, Lyft and friends pick you up?

The official end-of-event passenger pickup location is the Jurassic Lot, just inside Entrance 2 off Highway 93. Red Rocks tells concertgoers to follow staff directions and walk downhill; the recommended route is via the Will Call Trail across from the Trading Post and down Red Rocks Park Road.

Is there a bus or train home from Red Rocks?

Do not assume a rail stop named “Red Rocks College” means a train serves the amphitheatre. It does not put you at the venue. We are building a separate public-transit guide because this question deserves a clean explanation rather than a one-line answer.

Can you walk somewhere after a Red Rocks concert?

You should not treat Red Rocks like a downtown entertainment district where a hotel, train station or dense cluster of businesses is automatically a safe sidewalk walk away. We are researching the specific pedestrian connections before publishing route advice. For leaving the venue to meet a pickup, follow Red Rocks staff and the official Jurassic Lot directions.

Can I leave my car overnight?

Red Rocks says vehicles left overnight must be unoccupied, and vehicles not relocated before 10 AM the following day may be ticketed or towed. That makes “I'll just figure the car out tomorrow” a poor default plan.

Can I book just the ride home through Red Rocks DD?

Not as the standard Red Rocks DD product today. The current service is a prearranged round trip: pickup before the show, the licensed driver remains onsite, and the same trip takes your group home afterward. If you only need a post-show ride, rideshare, taxi, a friend pickup or another one-way provider may fit better.

What does Red Rocks DD actually solve?

It solves the planning problem. You know before the show that a licensed operator has the trip, the vehicle is matched to your group, and the driver stays onsite. You still share the road with everyone else when the venue empties, but you are not searching for transportation at that moment.
